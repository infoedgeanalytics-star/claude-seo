Slide 03 / 15 Part 1: Technical & On-Page SEO

Technical & On-Page Action Plan

Foundational fixes that unlock rankings — must be completed before scaling content

Technical SEO
  • Fix Core Web Vitals: compress images to WebP format, lazy-load below-fold images, defer non-critical JavaScript to improve LCP and FID scores
  • Canonical tags: implement canonical tags on all duplicate city/state service pages to consolidate link equity into primary URLs
  • Fresh XML sitemap: submit updated sitemap to Google Search Console including all service pages, blog posts, and city landing pages
  • Robots.txt audit: ensure no accidental blocking of CSS, JS files, or key service pages that prevent Googlebot from rendering pages
  • HTTPS everywhere: enable HTTPS on all sub-pages, fix mixed content warnings that downgrade security ratings
  • Hreflang implementation if targeting regional language audiences (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu content)
  • Fix 404s and redirect chains: use 301 permanent redirects; eliminate chains longer than 2 hops to preserve link equity
  • Performance stack: minify CSS/JS, enable browser caching, implement CDN (Cloudflare or equivalent) for sub-1s TTFB
On-Page SEO
  • Rewrite title tags: include primary keyword + brand name (e.g. “Trademark Registration in India | RegisterKaro”) — currently generic on most pages
  • Meta descriptions: write unique 150–160 character descriptions for all 50+ pages with a clear benefit and CTA
  • H1 audit: add H1 with primary keyword on every page (currently missing on ~40% of pages)
  • Keyword-rich subheadings: use H2/H3 tags to structure content and capture secondary and long-tail keyword variations
  • FAQ sections: add to all service pages targeting People Also Ask queries and voice search
  • Content depth: service pages should be 1,500–2,500 words with step-by-step guidance, not 300-word stubs
  • Above-fold CTAs: add clear calls-to-action — “Register Now”, “Get Free Consultation” — visible without scrolling
  • Trust signals: client count, Google ratings, certifications, MCA partner badge on all service pages
Priority Order
Week 1–2: Core Web Vitals + sitemap + robots.txt + canonical tags  |  Week 3–4: Title tags + meta descriptions + H1s + FAQ sections  |  Month 2: Content depth + trust signals + structured data

Slide 04 / 15 Part 1: Internal Linking, Content Gaps & CRO

Internal Linking · Content Gaps · Conversion Optimization

Three levers that turn traffic into leads — often overlooked, always high ROI

🔗 Internal Linking

  • Blog → Service Pages: Every blog post about trademark topics must link to /trademark-registration with descriptive anchor text (not “click here”)
  • Service cross-linking: Trademark → GST → Company Registration to mirror the user’s business journey and reduce bounce
  • Services Hub page: Create a central “All Services” page linking to all 8 service categories with brief descriptions
  • Breadcrumbs site-wide for improved crawlability, user orientation, and BreadcrumbList schema eligibility
  • Authority flow: identify top 10 highest-DR blog posts and add contextual links directly to conversion/service pages

📄 Content Gaps

  • No comparison pages: “LLP vs Pvt Ltd”, “Trademark Class Guide”, “TM vs Copyright vs Patent” — these are high-converting commercial pages
  • No city landing pages for trademark — Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad all have dedicated search volume
  • No calculator tools: Trademark Fee Calculator, Company Registration Cost Estimator — high-engagement lead capture tools
  • Missing post-registration content: “What happens after trademark registration?” — users search this actively
  • No social proof content: case studies, success stories, customer testimonials from real businesses

📈 Conversion Optimization

  • Sticky CTA bar: “Get Free Consultation” fixed at top or bottom on all service pages — always visible
  • Exit-intent popup triggered on scroll-up with lead magnet (free trademark registration checklist PDF)
  • Live chat / WhatsApp widget visible on all service pages — key for mobile users in India
  • Social proof counter: “10,000+ businesses registered” animated counter with logos
  • Simplified forms: max 3 fields — Name, Phone, Service (reduce friction, increase submissions)
  • Trust badges: ISO certification, Google Reviews star rating, MCA Partner logo above the fold
Quick Win: Internal Linking Audit
Run a Screaming Frog crawl to identify all orphan pages (pages with 0 internal links pointing to them). These pages receive no link equity and are often invisible to Google. Adding just 2–3 contextual internal links per orphan page can bring them back into Google’s crawl priority queue within weeks — often resulting in rapid ranking improvements for those pages.

Slide 08 / 15 Part 4: AEO Strategy

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Strategy

Capturing zero-click traffic: Featured Snippets, People Also Ask, and Voice Search

Featured Snippets Strategy

  • Definition queries: “What is trademark registration?” → 40–50 word direct answer at the very top of the page, before any elaboration
  • Process queries: “How to register a trademark in India?” → Use ordered numbered lists — Google strongly favors these for process-type snippets
  • Cost queries: “Trademark registration fees” → Present as a clean, scannable table with government fees vs professional fees
  • Inverted pyramid structure: Answer first, elaborate below. Never bury the answer inside paragraphs or after lengthy introductions
  • Page structure formula: H2 as question → Direct 40–60 word answer in <p> tag → Detailed elaboration and examples below
  • Target 40–60 words per snippet answer — this is the sweet spot for Google’s paragraph snippet extraction algorithm

People Also Ask (PAA) Strategy

  • Research PAA boxes for each target keyword using live SERPs and tools like AlsoAsked.com or semrush PAA feature
  • Map top 10 PAA questions per service page — answer all of them directly on the page in FAQ format
  • Collapsible FAQ section at bottom of every service and blog page (accordion UI is ideal)
  • Answer length: 40–60 words per PAA question — concise but complete and self-contained
  • Example PAA targets for Trademark page:
  • · How much does trademark registration cost in India?
  • · How long does it take to get a trademark registered?
  • · Can I register a trademark myself in India?
  • · What documents are needed for trademark registration?

Voice Search Optimization

  • Conversational long-tail queries: “Hey Google, how do I register my brand name in India?” — write FAQ answers in natural spoken language
  • Natural language in FAQ answers: write how people speak, not how they type — contractions, complete sentences, no jargon
  • Local voice search: optimize for “trademark registration near me” — requires verified Google Business Profile with accurate location
  • Google Business Profile: ensure complete verification with photos, services list, Q&A section, and regular posts
  • Superlative queries: target “best/cheapest/fastest trademark registration in India” with direct comparison content and clear value proposition
  • Mobile-first imperative: voice search is predominantly mobile — Core Web Vitals must pass for voice eligibility
AEO Opportunity: Featured Snippet Domination
For “what is trademark registration” (8,100/mo), “how to register a trademark in india” (5,400/mo), and “trademark registration fees india” (1,400/mo) — these are active featured snippet opportunities. Winning just these 3 snippets = ~7,000+ additional impressions/month. Featured snippet clicks average 8–12% CTR compared to 3–5% for a normal position 1 result.

Slide 09 / 15 Part 4: Schema & Content Formatting

Schema Markup · Content Formatting · AI Overview Optimization

Structured data and formatting signals that unlock rich results and AI citations

Recommended Schema Types for RegisterKaro
  • FAQPage schema → for FAQ section on every service page (boosts PAA appearance and rich result eligibility)
  • LocalBusiness schema → NAP, business hours, service area, geo-coordinates for local SEO
  • Service schema → service name, price range, provider, areaServed attribute
  • BreadcrumbList schema → for navigation trail on all pages (improves SERP display)
  • Review / AggregateRating schema → for testimonials to show Google star ratings in search results
  • HowTo schema → for “How to register a trademark in India” step-by-step guide pages
  • Organization schema → on homepage with logo, social profiles, founding date, contact info
Content Formatting Strategy
  • Quick Answer box: open every page with a 2-sentence highlighted callout answering the primary query immediately
  • H2s as questions: “What is Trademark Registration?” “How Much Does It Cost?” — Google extracts these directly
  • Short paragraphs: keep to ≤3 sentences for mobile readability and AI content extraction
  • Bullet lists: use for steps, requirements, and features — Google strongly favors list-formatted content for snippets
  • Fee comparison tables: present all cost data in table format for snippet eligibility
  • Key Takeaways box: add near top — AI tools and featured snippets love structured summary boxes
  • TL;DR summary: add at page bottom for AI crawlers and time-poor mobile readers
Quick Answer Box Example

“Trademark registration in India costs ₹4,500–₹9,000 in government fees (individual/startup vs others) plus professional fees starting at ₹1,499. The process takes 18–24 months for full registration, but the TM symbol can be used immediately after filing.”

Google AI Overview Optimization
  • Clear topic sentences: the first sentence of every paragraph must state the main point — AI models extract these for summaries
  • Cite authoritative sources: link to IP India, MCA, Ministry of Commerce — signals credibility to AI ranking systems
  • Specific data points: “18–24 months” is citable; “several months” is not — AI prefers specific, verifiable claims
  • E-E-A-T compliance: include author credentials, professional qualifications, and last-updated date on every article page
  • Sources section: add a “References” section at page bottom — mirrors academic citation style that AI training data prefers
E-E-A-T Quick Fix
Add an author bio box to every article: include the author’s name, qualification (e.g. “CS, 10 years in corporate law”), LinkedIn URL, and a 2-line bio. This single change has been shown to significantly improve content’s AI Overview inclusion rate and overall E-E-A-T score in Google’s quality assessment.
