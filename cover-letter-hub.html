What Is a Cover Letter & Why Does It Matter?

A cover letter is a one-page document sent alongside your resume when applying for a job. It introduces you to the hiring manager, highlights your most relevant skills, and explains why you're the right fit for the role. A well-written cover letter can set you apart from hundreds of applicants — even when qualifications look similar on paper.

  • Personalises your application beyond what a resume shows
  • Demonstrates genuine interest in the company and role
  • Gives you space to address gaps, career changes, or unique circumstances
  • Showcases communication and writing ability — both prized by employers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a cover letter necessary when applying for a job in India?
While not always mandatory, a cover letter significantly strengthens your application. Many Indian recruiters — especially in MNCs, startups, and senior roles — actively look for one. It shows initiative and allows you to personalise your pitch in a way a resume cannot.
How long should a cover letter be?
Ideally 250–400 words — roughly three to four short paragraphs on one page. Anything longer risks losing the reader's attention. Focus on quality over quantity: every sentence should add value.
What is the difference between a cover letter and a resume?
A resume is a structured list of your experience, skills, and qualifications. A cover letter is a narrative document that explains your motivation, connects your background to the specific role, and shows personality. They work together — the resume lists; the cover letter sells.
Should I write a different cover letter for each job?
Yes — always customise. At a minimum, change the company name, the role title, a specific reason you want to work there, and the key skills you match to the job description. Generic cover letters are easy for recruiters to spot and are far less effective.
What font should I use in a cover letter?
Stick to professional, readable fonts: Calibri, Arial, Helvetica, or Georgia at 10–12pt. Avoid decorative fonts. Ensure consistent formatting with your resume so both documents look like a matched set.
Can freshers skip the cover letter?
Freshers benefit the most from cover letters. With limited work experience, the cover letter is your best opportunity to highlight academic projects, internships, extracurricular achievements, and genuine enthusiasm — all things a resume struggles to convey well.
