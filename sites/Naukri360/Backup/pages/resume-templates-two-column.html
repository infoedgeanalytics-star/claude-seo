What Is a Two-Column Resume Format?

The two-column resume format is a format that separates your resume into two columns. The primary column normally includes the work experience and the accomplishments, whereas the secondary one includes skills, contact information, and certifications.
This structure enhances organization and gives employers a chance to review your profile within a short period. A two-column CV format with a good design will ensure that your resume creates an impression of being modern, professional, and easy to navigate.
An average two-column resume includes:
Left column: Skills, personal contact details, certifications, and technological devices.
Right column: Your work experience and education, and big accomplishments.
You will be able to easily find your most important certifications thanks to this design.
