Frequently asked questions

Is the ATS resume checker really free?
Yes. The full score, category breakdown, and fix-list are free with no sign-up. You can run unlimited checks.
What does an "ATS score" actually mean?
It's a 0–100 estimate of how well your resume will be parsed and ranked by a typical Applicant Tracking System. Scores ≥80 are considered recruiter-ready; 60–79 needs minor fixes; below 60 will likely be filtered out.
What file types do you support?
PDF, DOC and DOCX, up to 5 MB. We recommend exporting from Word as PDF for the cleanest result. Image-only PDFs (scanned files) cannot be parsed by any ATS.
Do you store my resume?
No. Files are processed in memory and discarded as soon as the score is generated. We do not retain, share, or sell your data.
Why does pasting a job description help?
It lets us compare your resume's keywords, skills and experience against the exact role you're targeting — making the "keyword match" score much more accurate.
How is this different from spell-check or grammar tools?
Spell-checkers look at language. We look at structure — whether an ATS can read your resume at all, find the right sections, and surface you for the right keywords.
