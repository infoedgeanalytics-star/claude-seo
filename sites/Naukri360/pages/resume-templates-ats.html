What Are ATS Resume Templates and Why They Matter

ATS Resume Templates are resume formats that are easy to read by the Applicant Tracking Systems. These systems search resumes to find valuable information, including contact, skills, education, and job experience.ATS software makes the resumes prioritized according to their fit with the job description, and only the resumes that are the best are forwarded to the next step. ATS-friendly Resume Templates will be the right way to make sure that your resume will have the proper structure and keywords that will be necessary to complete the ATS screening process. ATS resume templates enhance:
  • ATS software readability
  • Matching keywords with job descriptions
  • Visibility to recruiters
  • Shortlist possibilities
This is why ATS resume templates are useful for today’s job search.

Key Features of ATS-Friendly Resume Templates

ATS friendly Resume Templates contain valuable characteristics that help your resume pass through the ATS screens.
  1. Limited and simple design: ATS prefers simple designs and headings. It should not have any graphical content or an unorganized layout.
  1. Appropriate Section Headings: The ATS resume format should have the following section headings:
  • Contact Information
  • Professional Summary
  • Work Experience
  • Skills
  • Education
  1. Keyword Optimization: The ATS system reads keywords from the job description in the resume. Keywords in the resume are easier to use with the help of ATS resume templates.
  1. ATS-Compatible File Types: ATS-compatible resume files often have the extension.doc or.docx.

Tips to Create ATS-Friendly Resume Templates

Ways to make your resume ATS-friendly:
  • Keep the formatting simple and clean
  • Do not include graphics, tables, or images
  • Include relevant keywords
  • Keep the headings simple
  • Follow the ATS friendly resume format
These tips will help your resume pass the ATS test.
